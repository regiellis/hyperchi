Everything on this page is free to use in coverage, previews, and videos about
our games. You do not need to ask first. If you need something that is not
here, write to the press address and we will answer within two working days.

## Short description

Driftlamp is a three-person studio making small, careful games about keeping
things running. Its games are *Drystone* (2021), *Pocket Orrery* (2023), and
*The Lamplit Crossing*, entering Early Access in spring 2027.

## Review copies and streaming

We send review keys for every release to outlets and creators of any size.
Streaming and monetized videos of all our games are allowed, with no need to
credit us beyond naming the game.
