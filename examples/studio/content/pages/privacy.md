---
title: Privacy
description: What this site stores about you, why, and how to have it removed.
---

This site stores as little as it can.

## The newsletter

When you join the list, we store your email address, which emails you chose,
and the time you signed up, in a database on our server. That is all. This
example site never sends email, so nothing is sent to that address.

To be removed, write to privacy@driftlamp.example from the address you signed
up with. We delete it within seven days and confirm when it is gone.

## Everything else

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party scripts
or fonts. Your browser keeps one value in local storage: your light or dark
theme choice, which never leaves your device.

Our server logs each request (the address, the page, and the time) for
troubleshooting and deletes the logs after fourteen days.
