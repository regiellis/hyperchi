*The Lamplit Crossing* carries passengers between eleven islands, and each one
has a story that changes with what you bring them. We need a writer to finish
the passengers of the last four islands and to write new letters for each
Early Access update.

## What you will do

- Write and revise passenger stories in our branching script format.
- Work with Mara on how each story changes the islands' requests.
- Read player feedback after each update and fix what does not land.

## What we are looking for

- Published interactive writing: a game, interactive fiction, or a narrative podcast.
- Comfort with short forms. Most passenger scenes are under 300 words.
- Interest in ordinary lives: ferry passengers are nurses, piano tuners, and children going to school.

## Terms

A fixed monthly fee agreed before you start, paid on the first of each month.
You keep the right to show your work in a portfolio, and you are credited in
the game.

## How to apply

Send a short note and two writing samples to jobs@driftlamp.example. We reply
to every application within two weeks.
