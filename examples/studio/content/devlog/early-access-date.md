*The Lamplit Crossing* enters Early Access in spring 2027. We will announce the
exact day six weeks ahead, once certification for the Steam Deck is done.

## What is in the first release

- Seven of the eleven islands, and the full first half of the winter.
- Every system: routes, lamps, cargo, passengers, weather.
- Controller support, text scaling, and captions for every sound.

## What comes after

The remaining four islands arrive in two updates, each with its own stretch of
the story. We expect Early Access to last about nine months. Saves carry over
through every update.

## Price

We have not set it. It will not go up when the full game releases, and
everyone who buys during Early Access gets the finished game without paying
again.
