Every night the ferry *Marguerite* leaves Hollin Pier with whatever the
islands need: post, medicine, a piano, a family moving house. You decide the
route, the cargo, and how much lamp oil to burn. The sea decides the rest.

## Run the crossing

Each night is a short planning phase and a slow, tense voyage. Fog banks drift
on currents you learn to read. Lanterns burn down. A late passenger can make
you miss the tide at Skerry Low, and the next night's route bends around that
choice.

- Plan routes across eleven hand-drawn islands, each with its own tides and harbor rules.
- Trim, refill, and ration lamp oil. Light is how you see, and how the islands see you.
- Carry people as well as cargo. Their stories change what the islands ask of you.

## A winter that remembers

The crossing runs for one winter, about twelve hours of play. Islands
remember what you brought and what you left on the pier. There is no fail
screen: a bad night costs trust, oil, and time, and the season carries on.

## Made for the Deck and for small hours

The whole game plays with a controller, text scales to 200 percent, and every
sound cue has a caption. A voyage can be paused at any moment and resumed on
another device from a 2 KB save.
