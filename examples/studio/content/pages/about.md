Driftlamp is a game studio of three people. We started in 2019 in a rented
room above a chandlery and now work mostly from home, meeting at a shared desk
two days a week. We have shipped two games, *Drystone* and *Pocket Orrery*, and
we are making a third, *The Lamplit Crossing*.

## What we make

Small games about keeping things running: a wall, a clock, a ferry. They are
quiet, they respect your time, and they can be put down in the middle and
picked up a week later without a recap.

## The team

**Mara Ilves**, design and code. Founded the studio after eight years writing
logistics software, which explains a lot about our games.

**Tobi Achterberg**, art and animation. Paints every scene by hand, including
the light.

**June Okoro**, sound, music, and production. Keeps the schedule honest and the
harbor sounding like a harbor.

## How we work

- We ship when the game is ready and say so early when a date moves.
- We pay contractors on time, in full, and credit them in the game.
- No crunch. Our hours are posted on the office door and we keep them.
- Every game ships with controller support, text scaling, and captions.

## What we will not do

No loot boxes, no paid currencies, no ads, and no telemetry beyond crash
reports you choose to send.

This studio is fictional: it is an example site for the HyperChi framework,
and the people, games, and numbers on it are invented.
