People ask how a studio of three pays for a game that takes three years. Here
is the plan we use, with round numbers.

## What a year costs

Salaries are most of it. We pay ourselves the same, monthly, and agree on the
figure each January. After salaries come contractors (writing, translation,
QA), software, hardware, a shared office desk two days a week, and an
accountant.

## Where the money comes from

Two sources this year: sales of *Pocket Orrery* and *Drystone*, which still
cover about a third of our costs, and an arts grant that covers a fifth. The
rest comes from savings we built after *Pocket Orrery* launched.

## The rule we keep

We always hold six months of costs in the bank. If the balance would drop below
that, we cut scope before we cut people. It has happened once, in 2024, and
the game was better for losing the features we cut.
