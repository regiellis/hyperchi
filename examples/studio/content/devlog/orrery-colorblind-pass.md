*Pocket Orrery* shipped in 2023 with gears told apart by color alone. That was
a mistake, and a player named Ines told us so, kindly, in a long email with
screenshots. Version 1.4 is out today and fixes it.

## What changed

Every gear family now has a shape cut into its hub: a circle, a square, a
triangle, or a star. The shapes match across all forty orreries, so you learn
them once.

We also raised the contrast of the brass against the felt workbench and added
a high-contrast workbench option.

## How we tested it

We sent builds to eight players with different kinds of color vision deficiency
and watched them play over video calls. The first version of the shapes was
too small to read on a Switch in handheld mode. The second version is twice
the size.

## What we do now

New games get the shape rule from the first prototype. Color can say something
extra; it never says something alone.
