An old clockmaker left forty orreries in pieces. Each one is a puzzle with a
single correct motion: the planets must keep their true periods, and the gears
must fit in the case.

## Gears, ratios, and nothing else

There are no timers and no score. You fit gears on spindles, count teeth, and
turn the crank. When the ratios are right, the brass planets settle into their
orbits and the case chimes.

- Forty orreries in four workshops, from a two-planet toy to a full planetarium.
- A free build mode with every part unlocked.
- Version 1.4 adds a colorblind pass: every gear carries a shape as well as a color.

## Reception

*Pocket Orrery* sold through its first print of 300 boxed Switch copies in a
week, and players have finished more than a million orreries since launch.
