We test our games with players who use controllers only, screen magnifiers,
captions, and color filters, and we want to do it more often. This role plays
our weekly builds and reports what blocks, slows, or confuses.

## What you will do

- Play the weekly build with the settings you use every day.
- Report problems with clear steps and, where you can, a save file.
- Retest fixes and tell us when something is actually better.

## What we are looking for

- Lived experience with assistive settings in games, of any kind.
- Clear written reports. Testing experience helps but is not required.
- A computer or Steam Deck that runs a small 2D game.

## Terms

Paid hourly, monthly, at a rate agreed before you start. Hours are flexible
week to week. You are credited in the game.

## How to apply

Write to jobs@driftlamp.example and tell us how you play. No CV needed.
