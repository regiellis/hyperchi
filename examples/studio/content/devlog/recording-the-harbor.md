The harbor in *The Lamplit Crossing* sounds like a real harbor because most of
it is one. I spent eleven nights this winter on a fishing pier with a recorder
and too few layers of wool.

## Why four in the morning

That is when the pier is quietest. No traffic, no gulls, no radio from the
boats. You hear the water slap the pilings, the ropes creak, and a buoy bell
somewhere out in the dark. Those three sounds carry most of a night crossing.

## From recordings to the game

The raw files are long and noisy. I cut each one into short loops, removed
clicks and wind bumps, and tagged them by intensity. The game mixes four to six
loops at a time and crossfades them as the weather changes.

## The part I did not expect

Silence is hard to record. The quiet between waves on the pier is full of
distant hum. For the stillest moments in the game I used a recording made in a
closet, under a duvet, of nothing at all.
