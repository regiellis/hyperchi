*Drystone* was our first game. You walk a hillside with a cart of stones and
mend the field walls that hold it together. Nobody times you. The walls hold
or they fall.

## Read the stone

Every stone has faces, a length, and a weight. Long stones bind the wall;
small ones fill the hearting. Place a stone badly and the course above it
leans. Place it well and the wall will outlast you.

- Twelve fields across one valley, each with its own stone.
- A gentle physics model that shows you why a wall failed.
- Made with advice from two working wallers in the hills above our old office.
