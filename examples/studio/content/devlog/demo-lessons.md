The demo ran for one week in July. A little over two thousand people played
it, and about four hundred sent feedback through the in-game form. Thank you,
all of you.

## The numbers

- Median session: 41 minutes, longer than the demo's single night.
- 62 percent of players finished the demo night.
- The most replayed moment: the fog bank outside Skerry Low.

## What surprised us

Players read the map far more than we expected, and the map was the least
finished part of the demo. Many people also played with sound off, which hid
the creaking ropes that warn you the ferry is overloaded.

## Three changes

1. The map gets a second pass before Early Access.
2. Overloading now shows on the hull as well as in the sound.
3. The first night is shorter, so the opening reaches the sea sooner.
