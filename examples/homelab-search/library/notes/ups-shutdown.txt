UPS shutdown order
==================

The UPS is an APC Smart-UPS 1500 on USB to the NAS. The NAS runs the NUT
server; everything else is a NUT client in netclient mode.

On battery, after 5 minutes or at 40% charge, whichever comes first:

  1. atlas stops its containers (compose down) and powers off.
  2. pve1 and pve2 shut down their guests, then power off.
  3. The NAS powers off last, then tells the UPS to cut the load.

Power returns: the NAS boots on AC restore (BIOS setting). Proxmox hosts
boot by Wake-on-LAN from the NAS script /usr/local/bin/wake-servers.

Test this twice a year: pull the UPS plug, watch the logs, time it.
Last test: shutdown sequence took 3 min 40 s. Battery runtime at idle load
is about 22 minutes.
